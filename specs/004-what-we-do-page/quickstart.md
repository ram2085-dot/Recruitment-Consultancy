# Quickstart: Validating the What We Do Page

## Prerequisites

- The What We Do page has content authored per FR-001–FR-003: services list, featured
  Transformer industry, secondary industries.

## Validation scenarios

| # | Action | Expected Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Load What We Do | Permanent Staffing and Executive Search are listed, each with a description |
| 2 | Load What We Do | Transformer industry is visually distinct (heading/callout) from the secondary list |
| 3 | Load What We Do | Secondary industries are listed below/alongside the featured one |
| 4 | Scroll to the end | CTA links to Contact Us or For Employers |
| 5 | Load on a 375px-wide viewport | All sections stack without horizontal scroll |

## Done when

All 5 scenarios pass.
